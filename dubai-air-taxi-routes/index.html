Dubai Air Taxi Routes, Vertiports & Travel Times

Dubai's urban air mobility network connects key aviation, coastal, and financial hubs using all-electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) aircraft cruising at up to 320 km/h. With both the DXB Airport (VDX) and Dubai Marina (VMR) vertiports certified by the UAE General Civil Aviation Authority (GCAA), the emirate's first permanent point-to-point air taxi corridor is established ahead of commercial passenger launch.
