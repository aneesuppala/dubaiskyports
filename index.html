          7 Official RTA Fare Zones        
       
                              
           
              NOL ZONE 1               South Hub            
           

Jebel Ali & Expo District

           

              Southern commercial, logistics, and residential corridor bordering Abu Dhabi.            

           
             
Key Communities: JAFZA, Expo City Dubai, Dubai South, Al Furjan, Discovery Gardens, JVC, JVT, Dubai Investment Park (DIP).
             
Metro Stations: Expo 2020, UAE Exchange, Danube, Energy, Ibn Battuta, Discovery Gardens, Al Furjan.
           
         
                   
           
              NOL ZONE 2               Coastal & Marina            
           

Marina, Palm & Barsha

           

              Prime coastal, hospitality, and residential waterfront stretch of southwestern Dubai.            

           
             
Key Communities: Dubai Marina, JBR, Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah Lake Towers (JLT), Al Barsha, Al Sufouh, Dubai Internet City, Media City, Emirates Hills.
             
Metro & Tram: DMCC, Sobha Realty, Al Khail, Dubai Internet City, Mashreq, Mall of the Emirates, Dubai Tram Network.
           
         
                   
           
              NOL ZONE 3               Central Core            
           

Downtown, DIFC & Al Quoz

           

              Metropolitan financial core, high-density residential towers, and central design districts.            

           
             
Key Communities: Downtown Dubai, DIFC, Business Bay, Al Quoz Industrial, Al Safa, Jumeirah 1-3, Al Wasl, Satwa.
             
Metro Stations: Equiti, Onpassive (Al Safa), Business Bay, Burj Khalifa / Dubai Mall, Financial Centre, Emirates Towers, World Trade Centre.
           
         
                   
           
              NOL ZONE 4               Inland Suburbs            
           

Silicon Oasis, Academic City & Dubailand

           

              Eastern inland suburban communities, university clusters, and technology parks.            

           
             
Key Communities: Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO), Dubai Academic City, International City, Nad Al Sheba, Meydan, Arabian Ranches, Motor City, Dubai Hills Estate.
             
Transit: RTA Dedicated Feeder Bus Networks (Upcoming Metro Blue Line connection by 2029).
           
         
                   
           
              NOL ZONE 5               Historic Creek            
           

Deira, Bur Dubai & DXB Airport

           

              Historic trade settlements, souks, governmental headquarters, and international airport terminals.            

           
             
Key Communities: Bur Dubai, Deira, Karama, Al Mankhool, Al Garhoud, Al Jaddaf, Dubai Creek Harbour, Al Rigga, Al Ras.
             
Metro Stations: Union, BurJuman, ADCB, Max (Al Jafiliya), Airport Terminal 1 & 3, Deira City Centre, Al Rigga, Al Ghubaiba.
           
         
                   
           
              NOL ZONES 6 & 7               Northern & Mountain            
           

Al Qusais, Muhaisnah & Hatta Enclave

           

              Northern border districts leading into Sharjah, plus the southeastern Hatta mountain territory.            

           
             
Zone 6 Communities: Al Qusais, Muhaisnah, Al Twar, Al Nahda (Dubai side), Al Khawaneej, Mirdif, Al Warqa'a.
             
Zone 7 (Mountain): Hatta Exclave, Hatta Heritage Village, Hatta Dam (Hatta Express Bus H02 / H04).
             
Metro Stations: Centrepoint, Rashidiya, Etisalat by e&, Al Qusais, Dubai Airport Free Zone (DAFZA).
           
         
       
     
           
       
         

            UAE Regional Transit Guide (All 7 Emirates)          

          Interstate Public Transport Map        
       
                              
           
              EMIRATE 1               ITC / Hafilat System            
           

Abu Dhabi (Capital)

           

              Primary intercity links run via Sheikh Zayed Road (E11) and the upcoming Etihad Rail passenger terminal in MBZ City.            

           
             
Major Corridors: Abu Dhabi Island, Mussafah, Khalifa City, Yas Island, Al Ain, Al Dhafra.
             
Dubai Express Buses:                  Route E100 (Al Ghubaiba ⇄ AD Central),                  Route E101 (Ibn Battuta ⇄ AD Central),                  Route E102 (Ibn Battuta ⇄ Mussafah).              
             
Local Fare Media: Hafilat Smart Card (Standard local trips AED 2).
           
         
                   
           
              EMIRATE 2               RTA / Nol System            
           

Dubai

           

              Multi-modal urban transit core featuring automated metro lines, street trams, marine abras, electric buses, and upcoming commercial vertiports.            

           
             
Major Corridors: SZR, Deira, Bur Dubai, Business Bay, Marina, JAFZA, Creek Harbour, Dubai South.
             
Core Transit: Red & Green Metro Lines, 1,500+ city buses, Dubai Water Taxi, Etihad Rail Al Yalayis station.
             
Local Fare Media: Unified Nol Card (Silver, Gold, Blue, Red).
           
         
                   
           
              EMIRATE 3               SRTA / Sayer System            
           

Sharjah

           

              High-volume interstate commuter artery with extensive coach connections operating out of Al Jubail Bus Station.            

           
             
Major Corridors: Al Majaz, Al Qasimia, Muwaileh, University City, Al Dhaid, Khorfakkan, Kalba.
             
Dubai Express Buses:                  Route E303 (Union ⇄ Al Jubail),                  Route E306 (Al Ghubaiba ⇄ Al Jubail),                  Route E307 (Deira City Centre ⇄ Al Jubail).              
             
Local Fare Media: Sayer Smart Card & Nol compatibility on interstate buses.
           
         
                   
           
              EMIRATE 4               Ajman Transport / Masaar            
           

Ajman

           

              Direct highway coach services linking Al Musalla Bus Station with central Dubai and northern coastal emirates.            

           
             
Major Corridors: Ajman Corniche, Al Rashidiya, Al Nuaimiya, Jurf Industrial, Masfout enclave.
             
Dubai Express Buses:                  Route E400 (Union Square ⇄ Ajman Al Musalla, ~AED 12 via Nol),                 Route E411 (Etisalat Metro ⇄ Ajman).              
             
Local Fare Media: Masaar Card (Ajman local network).
           
         
                   
           
              EMIRATE 5               RAKTA / Saqr Card            
           

Ras Al Khaimah

           

              Northern mountain and coastline emirate connected via Mohammed bin Zayed Road (E311) and Emirates Road (E611).            

           
             
Major Corridors: Al Nakheel, Al Hamra Village, Marjan Island, Jebel Jais.
             
Dubai Express Buses: RAKTA Intercity Express connects directly from Al Hamra Bus Station to Union Metro Station (AED 25-30).
             
Local Fare Media: Saqr Card (RAK Transport Authority).
           
         
                   
           
              EMIRATES 6 & 7               East Coast & North Coast            
           

Fujairah & Umm Al Quwain

           

              East coast Gulf of Oman hub (Fujairah) and tranquil northern coastal hub (Umm Al Quwain).            

           
             
Fujairah Transit: Route E700 (Union Metro ⇄ Fujairah Central, AED 30 Nol). Future Etihad Rail passenger hub at Al Hilal City.
             
Umm Al Quwain Transit: Intercity express coaches connecting King Faisal Road / UAQ Bus Stop to Dubai Union Station.
             
Transit Access: Nol card accepted on RTA-operated intercity bus corridors.

            Future Rail Expansion            

Dubai Metro Blue Line (September 2029)

           

              AED 20.5B strategic rail line adding 30 km (15.5 km underground, 14.5 km elevated) and 14 new stations.            

         
         
           
• Key Interchanges: Creek (Green Line) & Centrepoint (Red Line)
           
• Communities Served: Dubai Creek Harbour, Silicon Oasis, Academic City, International City 1 & 2
           
• Landmark Viaduct: 1.3 km bridge crossing directly across Dubai Creek
