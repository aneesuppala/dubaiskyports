Nol Zone Calculation Notes

Dubai's unified public transport system (Metro, Tram, and Buses) is billed across 7 standard geographic Nol tariff zones. Trips confined to 1 zone are charged at AED 3.00, trips traversing 2 adjacent zones are AED 5.00, and journeys passing through 3 or more zones are billed at the maximum tier of AED 7.50 on a standard Silver Nol card. Transfers completed within 30 minutes across modes count as a single journey.
