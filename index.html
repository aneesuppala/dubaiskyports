Network Expansion Project

Dubai Metro Blue Line (Opening September 2029)

The AED 20.5 billion strategic expansion spanning 30 km (15.5 km underground, 14.5 km elevated) and adding 14 stations across key high-density districts.

Dual Branch Y-Junction

Branch A runs 21 km from Creek Interchange (Green Line) to Academic City. Branch B runs 9 km from Centrepoint (Red Line), meeting at International City 1.

Key Connected Communities

Provides direct rail access to Dubai Creek Harbour (featuring a landmark 74m tall station), Dubai Silicon Oasis, International City, and Academic City.

Creek Crossing Landmark

Features the Dubai Metro’s first bridge crossing directly over Dubai Creek via a 1.3 km architectural viaduct, cutting travel from Creek to Academic City to ~25 minutes.

Future Network Expansions & High-Speed Corridors

Etihad Rail’s long-term master plan involves deeper urban integration into Dubai and high-speed connections across the Arabian Gulf.

4 Dubai Stations Plan

In addition to Al Yalayis (JGE), future planned Dubai stations include Al Jaddaf (connecting to Dubai Metro Green & Blue Lines) and Meydan (connecting to the upcoming Gold Line).
